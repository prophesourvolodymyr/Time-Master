## Section format (same as the old camp builder, see `~/.tm-ingest/bin/camp_build.py` `section_ops`)
- Plain section: `{"page": "<library path or exact title>", "name": "<label shown in the player, optional>", "duration": <seconds per set>, "sets": n, "repCount": n, "restBetweenSets": s, "restAfter": s, "prepareTime": s, "untimed": True/False}`.
- Bundle (several exercises back to back, N rounds): `{"name": "...", "bundle": {"exercises": [{"page": "...", "name": "...", "duration": s, "repCount": n, "restAfter": s}, ...], "sets": n, "restBetweenSets": s}, "restAfter": s, "untimed": True/False}`.
- `untimed: True` = no countdown, each set runs until the user taps Done: use it for rep-based work (pull-ups, dips, jumps, lunges, sprints counted in reps, rounds you count by feel). Holds, flows with seconds, stretches, rounds with a clock stay timed. `duration` on an untimed section is only the estimate for totals; give an honest estimate. With a bundle, `untimed` applies to every exercise in it.
- Workout-level `restBetweenSections` = 20 (seconds). Use `restAfter` on a section for a longer gap (for example 60 s after a hard block).
- Resolve pages with `camp_lib.Resolver` (it prefers pages with clips) or give exact paths. Find pages with `~/.tm-ingest/bin/libfind.py <regex> [<regex>...] [--kind exercise] [--clips]`. Prefer exercise pages that have a clip, from the folders where they belong (Calisthenics, Gymnastics, Boxing, Wrestling, Strength & Conditioning, Mobility & Flexibility). Never use a skill page unless the block is that skill. Do NOT invent library paths: if nothing fits, use the nearest page and say so in REPORT, or add a NEW_PAGES entry.
- Keep a workout to 4-8 sections (a flow is ONE bundle; the three-round finisher is ONE section, see boxing). Name sections so the player screen says what to do: e.g. "Explosive pull-ups 5x3" or "Planche lean 8 s + leg swings".
- Type (existing custom types): `Conditioning` (speed and plyo mornings, bike), `Gymnastics` (skill sessions, handstand, planche/lever, muscle-up, gymnastic strength), `Boxing`, `Wrestling`, `Mobility`, `Skill Work` (checkpoint tests only).
- Workout names: `W{n} {Day} {AM|PM} · {Title}` with Day in Mon..Sat, for example `W1 Mon AM · Plyos A + muscle-up 1`. Deload week 4 and taper week 7 add "(light)" to the title; test sessions are titled "Checkpoint tests".

## Rules from the research (obey)
- Morning sessions never contain deep static stretching. Deep stretches (pancake, pike, splits, hip flexor, shoulders, wrists, calves) only at the END of evening sessions or after the bike. Holds 45-60 s, 2 rounds where listed. PNF pancake (push 5-10 s at about half effort, relax, go deeper, 2-3 rounds) at most twice a week (Thu and Sat), other evenings easy holds.
- Between sets the user does a 30-45 s dynamic move (a "between-sets move" below, marked ↔) and the rest stays full: build it as a bundle [main exercise, dynamic move] with `restBetweenSets` = the specified rest minus the dynamic move's seconds. The dynamic move must not tire the muscle just worked (see the list).
- Skills and speed first in a session, fatigue later. No heavy leg work anywhere. Plyos at most 100 foot contacts per session. Live wrestling rounds with a partner not before week 5; weeks 1-4 are drilling and shadow only. No static stretch before sprints, plyos, handstand or boxing.
- One hard-day pattern: Mon, Tue, Wed hard; Thu light; Fri hard; Sat medium; Sun off.
- Pull-heavy work: muscle-up 1 (Mon), front lever (Wed PM, Fri short), muscle-up 2 (Sat). Never two pull-heavy blocks in one session except where the template says so. Planche (Wed PM) is never in the same session as handstand work except the 6 min easy balance listed there.

## 8-week progression (apply to the numbers above; keep it simple and say what you did in REPORT)
- Weeks 1-3 foundation: week 1 as written; week 2 and 3 add small steps only (examples: sprints 6 -> 7 -> 8; plyo contacts +10% by adding reps; holds 8 s -> 10 s -> 12 s; one more set on the main skill block; boxing rounds 5 -> 5 -> 6). Never raise more than one variable per block per week.
- Week 4 deload: about 60-65% volume (sets and reps down, same intensity, same session shapes), titles end "(light)". Saturday AM of weeks 1, 4 and 8 is `Checkpoint tests` instead of "Muscle-up 2 + handstand" (type Skill Work, 40 min): max handstand hold (2 tries), max strict pull-ups, front lever hold (best progression, 2 tries), broad jump x3, 20 m uphill sprint x2 (timed on the phone), press attempts; on those Saturdays the muscle-up 2 work is simply skipped.
- Weeks 5-6 specific: harder variants (advanced tuck, band-assisted straight-arm progressions, muscle-up attempts, reactive plyos such as lateral and reactive starts, boxing counters and ring craft themes, wrestling positional rounds with a partner from week 5 when he has one, otherwise shadow rounds); volume back to week-3 level, week 6 the peak (still plyos <= 100 contacts).
- Week 7 taper start: about 65% volume, intensity kept. Week 8: about 55-60% volume Mon-Fri, Saturday AM checkpoint tests, Saturday PM easy.
- Rep and hold targets always as numbers in the section (`repCount`, `duration`), never vague.
