## 4. Gaps this channel would fill

- **Planche progression.** The library has only Advanced Tuck, Full, Low Full, and negatives. There is no Tuck Planche, no Straddle Planche, no floor plank-lean drills. Get Gymnast Fit hyO2J0HqfPs gives a clean 11-drill ladder in one video.
- **Shoulder compression and scapular elevation for the straddle press.** The library press pages are pike and straddle drills. The shrug and compression chapters (D5hh_D813yc, hc5ny4YU5pQ, eLXmqorq1T0) are new.
- **Hanging core.** Only a few hanging pages exist. The wall-bar and hollow-hang drills are new.
- **Rings support and ring plank** (FkQHyiZKt1s).
- **Front lever.** No Front Lever page exists in the library, so Anton JbDaJ1Nwi-k is new.
- **Neck strength.** The library has only neck stretches. This matters for wrestling (OlSf-agVRi8).
- **Front split.** Only half split and lunge-to-half-split exist.
- **Feet** (toe-strength drills, lkPYNtjHG4A). Related foot drills (PVC Pipe Foot Rolling, Toe Stretch) exist.
- **Shoulder mobility sequences.** Ld6EJhb5NyA and 2Rz8LofcScA are numbered, with clean demos.
- **Boxing and wrestling conditioning.** This channel has no boxing content. Wrestling relevance is indirect (neck, spine, hips, posture).

The business and reaction videos are skip: f76-WT54B9g, trz-naZjqVI, -bUyw4IDxVI, iWkmgKauPHM, Fe-DaGgN8X8, YZy-uZ-Q22w, HsaXYjUTkbo, B2_32V3zh2U and similar titles, plus the case-study and programming talks (gzsu3hyelOc, jAc-Om0413c, 04iTLI5nMiQ).
