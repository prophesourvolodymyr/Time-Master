## Design Decisions to Preserve

- **Locked mode and target, no mode segmented control.** Appeal decision and Dispute result have different reasons and remedies. An immutable context prevents an outcome disagreement from silently becoming a nudity allegation or another account's appeal.
- **One safe original-decision card, not a gallery.** Show permitted original facts, the actual current-state change if any, date, and copyable reference. No body thumbnails, unsafe explanation quotes, media attachment, identity proof, or new camera request. The tradeoff is leaving visual comparison to an already authorized result owner rather than exposing it inside intake.
- **One document, not a wizard.** Required reason, optional note, visible recipient/retention consequences, and one mode-specific send stay in a single scrollable reading order. Secondary details expand inline. Do not meet minimal text by hiding material consequences.
- **iPhone gets writing height.** Use one native large-height sheet with Cancel and a safe-area send action; no half-height reason crop, stacked modal, fake phone chrome, fixed screen coordinates, or keyboard-obscured actions.
- **Mac is a real scoped window task.** Use one native window-attached sheet, comfortable readable width, normal title bar, native input density, bottom action row, preserved app commands, and other app windows remaining usable. No Catalyst/web dialog or permanently open new inspector for this short form.
- **The stage remains vertical.** If the underlying battle stage is present, keep timer first, all permitted badges grouped by owner above both LARGE players, You above Opponent at every width. Wrap all badges; scroll short windows instead of shrinking people, clipping rails, hiding +N, or putting badges over evidence.
- **Quiet tactile motion, never a verdict promise.** Shared press feedback, a 160-millisecond reason change, and one small receipt settle acknowledge real actions. No ambient loop, punishment theatre, re-award animation, body scan, or motion-only success.
- **The service remains authoritative.** A receipt is not human attention, an appeal is not a new judge, and a pending request changes neither a restriction nor the effective result. Corrections come only from the audited F29-C process.
