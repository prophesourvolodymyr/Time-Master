## Concrete Design Decisions and Tradeoffs

### Private dossier, not a rematch

The status surface contains a case-type symbol, the large actual state, one concise next-step sentence, and the last confirmed update. Latest decision follows when present. Your rating follows only when this case has competitive consequences. The newest Updates are quiet factual rows; Case facts and evidence is progressive metadata. Use the shared near-black, dark surfaces, Inter, and orange action language, not a parallel safety theme or punitive red hero.

This composition prioritizes action and accountability over imagery. It avoids a chat-like promise of a live moderator, a decorative progress ladder, and an additional route to reported bodies. No ambient loop or punishment celebration belongs on the dossier. Real state changes and tactile actions supply the requested animation.

### One real action, no filler CTA

Respond, Appeal decision, or Request scoring review is prominent only when actually applicable to this person's role and case. Passive states say No action needed now when true and do not invent a filled Continue button. Withdrawal is secondary and confirmed. Back or Close case does not withdraw a sent request. Support is discoverable without requiring access to a body image or authenticated case data when sign-in is unavailable.

### Separate review, decision, and settlement

A received request may be queued or under review. A withdrawn request may still require lawful safety handling. A closed case can retain a decision and an eligible appeal. A revised decision can precede settlement of the rating adjustment. Show these truths together instead of collapsing them into a single misleading progress enum. A technical fetch error never becomes Inconclusive, Closed, or No violation found.

### Native two-platform density

On iPhone, use normal navigation or the existing in-match safety presentation, a readable scroll column, and an inline response editor with keyboard-safe actions. On native Mac, retain the title bar and 800-by-600 minimum, a readable central dossier, native toolbar and focus, and optional supplementary metadata in a trailing inspector when space allows. At smaller widths the same metadata moves inline. Do not stretch a phone sheet across a desktop or make the inspector a second player panel.

### History and correction are different timelines

Original match values are historical. A later correction adjusts a then-current confirmed rating using service authority. Today's current rating may include still more matches. Distinct labels and settlement dates prevent a persuasive but false old-before-to-new-current animation. No numeric award roll is appropriate for a private administrative correction.
