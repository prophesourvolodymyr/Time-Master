# F29-A-e — Pose Guide

A fully local, illustrated reference for the three Physique Duels poses: front double-biceps, safe side physique, and rear double-biceps. It teaches comfortable positioning and comparable framing without taking a photo, examining a body, inventing a rival, or changing competitive state merely because someone reads it.

This is product context for future implementation, not an implemented guide, an accepted artwork set, a judging-quality claim, or permission to begin production code. The [page and match flow](../DOCKS.md) owns entry and the surrounding journey. The [Physique contract](../../DOCKS.md) owns competition behavior. The newer user direction requires native iPhone and native macOS, an animated minimal-text sports presentation, complete public badge rails above two large vertically stacked participants wherever a battle is present, and the real timer as its highest visual anchor.

One guide has two presentations: a camera-free reference opened before matchmaking, and contextual pose help inside an already active capture stage. ## Framing, Clothing, and Privacy Instructions

Framing & clothing is a native disclosure within the guide, not another custom screen or a permission gate. It contains the following complete guidance in short readable sections.

### Frame the whole pose

Place the device securely, far enough back for head, hands, elbows, and feet to remain visible together. Keep the camera roughly level and stable, with ordinary even lighting; avoid a steep up-angle, dramatic shadows, or placing one limb close to the lens to exaggerate size. No exact height, camera distance, device price, body weight, or pixel size is a scoring target. Move the device or framing before attempting an uncomfortable pose; do not step backwards blindly while facing away. There must be room to lower the arms and turn without hitting furniture.

The guide's whole-person example protects all required shoulder, upper-arm, upper-back, thigh, and calf evidence from cropping. It does not instruct a closer crop around an intimate region. The actual capture owner checks adequate evidence; no static frame illustration can declare a real capture accepted.

### Wear permitted, comfortable coverage

An opaque athletic top or opaque sports bra is permitted. A privately declared adult man may also pose shirtless in the feature's nonsexual fitness context. All participants keep the groin and buttocks covered by opaque sports clothing through every turn and pose. No one must expose more skin for a better score. Coverage must remain intact in the rear view, not only when facing the camera.

More visible skin is not an advantage. The common-region rubric concerns observable shoulder, upper-arm, upper-back and leg evidence; front-chest and abdominal appearance are excluded for both people. Breast, nipple, groin and buttock anatomy is not a judging region. Required upper-arm and calf evidence must be usable without inventing anatomy beneath fabric. If permitted clothing obscures required evidence, the capture flow may explain the issue and offer an ordinary safe adjustment or recapture within the existing limits. It must not demand removal of required coverage or make an unseen region a zero. The guide makes no promise that every permitted garment necessarily supplies sufficient evidence.

### Choose a private, safe space

Only the participant may appear. Check mirrors and the background for another person and identifying objects. A helper can position the device from outside the frame. A bystander invalidates a capture even if their face could be blurred or they say they consent.

The guide uses bundled illustrations only. It does not ask for a camera, microphone, Photos access, location, account login, or image-processing consent. Actual capture has separate eligibility and affirmative sharing decisions. Every transmitted candidate or selected still must have face areas obscured locally first, with an exact preview. Redaction failure stops that transfer; an original image is not a fallback. Masking does not guarantee anonymity, and permitted opponent stills cannot be made absolutely copy-proof. These facts are not replaced by the mannequin's featureless face or a decorative privacy symbol.
