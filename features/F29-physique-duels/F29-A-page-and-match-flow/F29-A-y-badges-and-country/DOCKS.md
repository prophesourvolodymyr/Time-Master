# F29-A-y — Badges and Country

## Purpose and Ownership

Battle identity is the participant's compact native view of what their public Physique identity means. It shows every earned launch badge, distinguishes current competitive standing from historical milestones, and lets the participant choose or remove an optional country or region. Country is the only editable value in this view. Earned badges, placement, tier, and streak are never local selections.

This is product context for a future connected iPhone and native macOS surface, not an implemented screen, accepted prototype, service-readiness claim, or human verification record. The coordinated F29 visual direction takes precedence over earlier optional horizontal comparisons. The launch badge meanings below follow the shared proposal; F29-F owns their trusted identity, publication, eligibility, and correction authority. F29-C remains the authority for competitive record meaning.

This child owns one custom Battle identity surface, its inline badge explanations, and its in-context native country picker. It does not own another country screen, an opponent-profile browser, avatar upload, a general Profile redesign, badge customization, or a new competition service. System sheets, alerts, pickers, and disclosure controls are reused rather than recreated as extra custom destinations.

## Optional Country or Region

The field begins as Not shown unless a real saved manual choice already exists. It never defaults to an inferred selection from location permission, GPS, IP address, device region, locale, language, SIM, time zone, photo, handle, face, or other account fields. Localization chooses the display names and sort order only. No location request, document, photo proof, nationality test, or citizenship claim is part of saving country.

The visible explanation before Save is: Optional and self-declared, not verified. Shown to paired opponents and on permitted public battle identity. Never used for judging. A short No location access line can accompany it. The field is not bundled with public-listing consent. Saving a region does not turn on a leaderboard listing, reveal an otherwise private profile, grant access to an opponent's stills, or alter adult/attire declarations. Public recipients remain limited by F29-F and the existing visibility rules.

Supported choices come from the shared country/region catalog. Display their complete localized names; the person is choosing a label, not certifying residence, nationality, or location. A supported flag emoji sits beside the permitted handle. If the platform cannot render the flag reliably, use a neutral globe/region symbol and retain the accessible region name. Not shown uses a neutral missing-country treatment, not a guessed flag. A saved retired or unrecognized selection says Saved region unavailable with its recognized safe name if available; the person can remove or replace it, never unknowingly save a substituted value.

Selecting, replacing, and removing change only a local draft until acknowledged Save. The preview is always labeled Preview, and Unsaved appears while the draft differs. The actual published identity rails continue to use confirmed permitted service state. A successful save makes the acknowledged value the saved baseline, removes Unsaved, and shows Saved inline. An unchanged draft cannot issue another save. Remove country is equally available without paying, earning a badge, or explaining why.

Confirmed removal removes the flag from this account's local permitted identity consumers immediately and respects the service's updated visibility on subsequent authorized displays, including battle/history identity projections. An older refresh cannot reinsert the removed flag. Country is not required competition evidence and is not pinned forever to a rating transaction. Removal cannot recall a screenshot, an already viewed flag, or an external copy. If service acknowledgement is unavailable, say Removal not confirmed rather than claiming remote erasure.

Country changes do not change matchmaking, Elo, score, model, judging order, badge qualification, allowance, result finality, or gallery permission. A change while a match exists is metadata only and never resets a deadline. Country, flags, badges, ratings, handles, and all UI chrome are excluded from both judge images and judge context. The judging path uses clean face-obscured evidence, never a screenshot of this view or the battle stage.
